Designing for Atmosphere

Atmosphere is the sum of decisions that never show up in a materials schedule: the height of a ceiling relative to the width of a room, the angle light enters at four in the afternoon, the sound a space makes when it's empty.

Salvia Modern House Museum's rooftop vinothèque and gold-trellis gallery atrium were both designed around this idea — the same building producing two entirely different emotional registers within a few floors of each other, simply by adjusting light, material, and enclosure.

Getting atmosphere right is less about adding features and more about restraint — knowing which single move (a slatted ceiling, a black resin pool, a courtyard water feature) will do the emotional work of an entire room.
