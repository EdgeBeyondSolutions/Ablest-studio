Studio Notes: From Sketch to Section

Every project in the studio starts the same way: understanding the site, the context, and the ambition behind it before a single line is drawn. That discovery stage shapes everything that follows.

From there, concept development, spatial refinement, and visualization run in parallel rather than in strict sequence — a section drawing might send a plan back to the drawing board, and a model built to test terrain (as with Terravista's cut-strip contour study) often reveals a structural idea no plan view could show.

The throughline across the studio's academic and built work — Asana Haus's early departure from orthogonal form, Emerald Spring Hill's move from framing to a finished arched arcade — is the same: keep testing an idea physically until it earns its place in the final design.
