When Light Becomes Architecture

Daylight is often treated as a technical problem to solve after the design is finished — glare control, energy code compliance, a line item. Treated as a design tool instead, orientation and light become some of the earliest decisions in a project, not the last.

La Raíz's arched bifacial photovoltaic canopy does double duty: it generates power and it choreographs the quality of light inside “La Plaza Viva” below, aiming toward a net-negative energy use intensity while shaping how the space feels to occupy.

Aquilo Flow pushes the same idea further, using a long, slatted pavilion to filter and layer light the way water filters through the Edwards Aquifer's limestone — light as material, not just illumination.
